# Service Ops Starter

A complete operations dashboard for a local service business, ready to be tailored to **your** business by AI.

This is not a demo. It is a copy of a real, battle-tested system that runs a multi-location auto glass company every day: lead intake, scheduling, customer text messages, technician pay, payments, review requests, callbacks, and bookkeeping checks. The auto-glass parts are clearly mapped so an AI assistant can strip them out and reshape the tool around your business — gutters, garage doors, locksmith, junk removal, appliance repair, or any business that sends workers to customers.

## What it does (out of the box)

- **Job pipeline** — intake a lead, schedule it, assign a worker, complete it, collect payment. One board shows what needs attention.
- **Automatic customer texts** — booking confirmations, night-before and morning-of reminders, job-complete and payment texts, receipts. English + Spanish supported.
- **Review engine** — a call-first review request flow that steadily fills your Google reviews.
- **Worker pay** — per-job pay, end-of-day settle-up, cash collection tracking, running balances.
- **Payments** — cash, Zelle, Venmo, check, and Stripe card links. Deposits and partial payments supported.
- **Callbacks / warranty visits** — track "come back and fix it" visits without losing them.
- **Daily digests** — a Telegram group message three times a day: what needs scheduling, what needs ordering, who hasn't paid.
- **Bookkeeping health** — optional bank feed (Plaid) that matches every deposit to a job, so nothing slips.

## Tech

Laravel 12 + Filament 4 (PHP 8.4, MySQL). Runs on any Laravel host for roughly $20/month. No prior coding needed — setup is driven by Claude (an AI assistant) following the script in this repo.
